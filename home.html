How it works

Built the way payment systems are actually built

1 — One call

Your platform creates a payment with a single authenticated, idempotent API request.

2 — Smart routing

The orchestration core selects a licensed provider — wallet, bank, acquirer or cash network — and executes through an isolated adapter.

3 — Signed truth

HMAC-signed webhooks propagate every state change. Settlement is executed by regulated partners; funds never touch Lebaneeds accounts.

Trust, by architecture

A technology company. Deliberately.

Lebaneeds Payments develops payment technology and financial infrastructure that enables businesses to integrate modern payment experiences through secure APIs and strategic partnerships with licensed providers.

We are not a bank, card issuer or licensed payment institution — and we never pretend to be. Regulated payment services and settlement are performed by licensed financial partners. Our job is the software.
