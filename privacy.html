Privacy Notice

How we handle your information

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

Our websites do not use analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising cookies. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us — through the partnership request forms (which compose an email from your own mail client) or by emailing us directly. That typically includes your name, company, work email address, and the contents of your message.

How we use it

We use the information you send solely to respond to your enquiry and, if a conversation continues, to manage the partnership or integration discussion you initiated. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where required to operate our email and hosting services or where the law requires it.

Third-party services

Our websites are hosted on Vercel and load fonts from Google Fonts; both services may process technical data such as your IP address as part of serving the page. Their own privacy policies govern that processing.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the discussion it belongs to. You can ask us at any time to correct or delete information you've sent us by writing to partners@lebaneeds.com.

Who we are

Lebaneeds Payments is a technology company, not a bank or licensed payment institution. Regulated payment services are provided by licensed financial partners. For anything related to this notice, contact partners@lebaneeds.com.
