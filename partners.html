Integration model

A dedicated adapter, built and maintained by us

We integrate against your existing APIs — you don't build anything new for us. Each provider gets an isolated adapter that speaks your protocol, your auth and your settlement model.

  • We build the adapter against your current API — no changes required on your side
  • Sandbox-first: full end-to-end testing before a single live transaction
  • Your risk rules and limits are enforced in the routing layer
  • Reconciliation files and reporting in the format your operations team already uses
  • Go-live gated by joint certification, not a calendar date

Compliance philosophy

Your license, your rules — our job is to respect both

We are a technology company, not a regulated financial institution — and we design so that boundary is never blurred.

  • Funds never touch Lebaneeds accounts — processing and settlement stay with licensed partners
  • Merchant KYB/KYC data collected to your standards and delivered to your compliance team
  • Your transaction limits, restricted categories and monitoring rules enforced at the routing layer
  • Complete, exportable audit trails for every transaction and administrative action
  • Clear contractual allocation of regulatory responsibilities before go-live
